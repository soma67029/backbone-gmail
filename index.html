Gmail Filter

A private family tool. It reads our own family's Gmail, read-only, to keep copies of our own bills, bank statements, tax and insurance papers in our own records.

  • It is not offered to the public and cannot be signed up for.
  • It only ever reads mail. It never sends, deletes, moves or changes anything in a mailbox.
  • Only family members who choose to sign in are read.
