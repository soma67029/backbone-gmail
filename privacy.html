Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026.

Gmail Filter is a private tool used only by one family for its own mail.

  • What it reads: mail in the Gmail accounts of family members who sign in, using Google's read-only permission (gmail.readonly).
  • What it does with it: keeps copies of the family's own documents (bills, statements, tax and insurance papers) on the family's own computers and storage.
  • What it never does: it never sends, deletes, moves, labels or marks mail; it never sells, shares or shows mail to anyone outside the family; it uses no advertising and no third-party analytics.
  • Google user data: its use follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Stopping it: anyone who signed in can remove its access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
